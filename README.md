# Department of Computer Science and Engineering
## Jagannath University

# Course Evaluation and Examination Policy



---







## 1. Course Evaluation Structure

The performance of students in each course shall be evaluated through a combination of **Continuous Assessment** and the **Final Examination**.

| Assessment Component | Weight |
|----------------------|:------:|
| Attendance & Class Performance | 10% |
| Assignment(s), Project(s), Presentation(s), Class Test(s), etc. | 10% |
| Mid-Term Examination | 20% |
| **Total Continuous Assessment** | **40%** |
| Final Examination | 60% |
| **Total Course Marks** | **100%** |

---


## 2. Attendance and Class Performance (10%)

Attendance and class performance shall carry **10%** of the total course marks.

### Attendance Mark Distribution

| Attendance Percentage | Marks (Out of 10) |
|:---------------------:|:-----------------:|
| 95% – 100% | 10 |
| 90% – below 95% | 9 |
| 85% – below 90% | 8 |
| 80% – below 85% | 7 |
| 75% – below 80% | 6 |
| 70% – below 75% | 5 |
| 65% – below 70% | 4 |
| 60% – below 65% | 3 |
| **Below 60%** | **Not Eligible** |

### Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students securing **less than 60% attendance** shall **not be eligible to appear in the Final Examination**.

However, under exceptional circumstances (e.g., serious illness, family emergency, participation in official university activities), a student may be permitted to sit for the examination subject to written approval from the appropriate academic authorities.

---

## 3. Assignment(s), Project(s), Presentation(s), Class Test(s), etc. (10%)

This component carries **10%** of the total course marks.

The course teacher may assess students through one or more of the following methods:

- Individual Assignment
- Group Assignment
- Programming Assignment
- Laboratory Exercise
- Project
- Seminar Presentation
- Viva Voce
- Class Test
- Quiz
- Problem Solving
- Case Study
- Any other assessment method relevant to the course

> **Note:** The number, format, and evaluation criteria of these assessments shall be determined by the respective course teacher.

---

## 4. Mid-Term Examination (20%)

The Mid-Term Examination shall carry **20%** of the total course marks.

### Mid-Term Examination Policy

- There will be **no department-wide fixed schedule** for Mid-Term examinations.
- Each course teacher shall independently determine the examination date and time.
- The examination format (written, practical, programming, oral, online, or hybrid) shall be decided by the respective course teacher.
- The syllabus, duration, and assessment method shall be communicated to students in advance.

---

## 5. Continuous Assessment (40%)

Continuous Assessment consists of:

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|:------:|
| Attendance & Class Performance | 10% |
| Assignment(s), Project(s), Presentation(s), Class Test(s), etc. | 10% |
| Mid-Term Examination | 20% |
| **Total** | **40%** |

Continuous Assessment is intended to evaluate students' participation, understanding, analytical ability, practical skills, and academic progress throughout the semester.

---

## 6. Final Examination (60%)

The Final Examination shall carry **60%** of the total course marks.

The examination shall be conducted according to:

- University Academic Calendar
- Departmental Academic Policies
- Jagannath University Examination Regulations

---

## 7. Final Examination Question Paper Preparation

To ensure fairness, transparency, and academic quality, the Final Examination question paper shall be jointly prepared by two faculty members.

| Examiner | Responsibility |
|----------|----------------|
| **Course Teacher (Internal)** | Prepare **60%** of the question paper |
| **External Faculty Member** | Prepare **40%** of the question paper |

The Department shall review and finalize the complete question paper before the examination.

---

## 8. Answer Script Evaluation

Each Final Examination answer script shall be evaluated independently by:

- Internal Examiner (Course Teacher)
- External Examiner

The student's Final Examination score shall be calculated as the **average of the marks awarded by the two examiners**.

---

## 9. Resolution of Significant Mark Differences

To ensure fairness and consistency:

- If the difference between the Internal Examiner's and External Examiner's marks is **20% or more** of the total examination marks,
- The answer script shall be evaluated by a **Second External Examiner (Third Examiner)**.

### Final Mark Calculation

1. Three independent marks shall be available.
2. The **two closest marks** shall be selected.
3. The student's Final Examination score shall be the **average of the two closest marks**.

---

## 10. Academic Integrity

Students are expected to maintain the highest standards of academic honesty.

Academic misconduct includes, but is not limited to:

- Plagiarism
- Cheating
- Fabrication or Falsification
- Unauthorized Collaboration
- Impersonation

Violations shall be dealt with according to the disciplinary rules and academic regulations of **Jagannath University**.

---

## 11. General Provisions

1. All examinations shall follow the academic regulations of Jagannath University.
2. The Department may issue additional assessment guidelines whenever necessary.
3. Matters not covered by this policy shall be resolved according to University regulations.
4. The Department reserves the right to amend this policy with approval from the appropriate academic authority.

---

## Effective Date

This policy shall become effective from the semester approved by the Academic Committee of the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Jagannath University**.
